# QurbSpace 0.2.0-beta.12

Faster sends. Installed copies update themselves to this one.

## Faster

- **A file from your computer reaches your phone in seconds.** With the phone
  connected and both on the same Wi-Fi, about 4 seconds from the computer
  telling the phone to *sent you a file*, measured on a Galaxy S23. It was
  28 seconds.
- **Sharing from the phone starts sending at once**, without first checking
  for updates or backing up folders.
- **The notice comes when the file does**, not after the phone has tried
  every other device.

## Downloads

Straight from the [README](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace#get-the-beta),
or:

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 8 or later | `QurbSpace-android.apk` |
| Linux | `QurbSpace-linux.AppImage`, or `QurbSpace-linux.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu |
| Windows 10 and 11 | `QurbSpace-windows-setup.exe`, **not yet run by us** |

## Known gaps

Unchanged from beta.11: deleting is for good; restoring a whole WhatsApp
backup to a new phone is not proven; Windows has not been run; Google Drive is
limited to the accounts added as testers until Google verifies the app. A
file waiting in Google Drive reaches the phone at its next sync: Drive cannot
wake a phone. Full list: [README](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace#known-gaps).

Something wrong? [Report it](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace/issues/new/choose).
