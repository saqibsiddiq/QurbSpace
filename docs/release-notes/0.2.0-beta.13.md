# QurbSpace 0.2.0-beta.13

Faster at home, and it fixes itself. Installed copies update themselves to
this one.

## Faster

- **Fast transfers at home.** A large file goes over a Wi-Fi link straight
  between your phone and your computer: 512 MB in about half a minute from tapping Send, three
  times as fast as through the router (measured on a Galaxy S23 and a Linux
  laptop). From the phone to the computer, when nobody is using the
  computer, which is offline for the minute it takes; otherwise the file goes
  the usual way, no slower. On
  in setup; in Advanced, *Fast link*. Android 13 or later, and a Linux or
  Windows computer.

## Fixed

- **Google Drive keeps working.** When Google ends a computer's sign-in (a
  password changed, access removed), your phone gives it a new one, with at
  most one tap.
- **The computer never stops syncing** because a device stops answering:
  every question gets two minutes, then the next.
- **A computer you are browsing shows as on**, not with a red dot.

## Downloads

Straight from the [README](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace#get-the-beta),
or:

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 8 or later | `QurbSpace-android.apk` |
| Linux | `QurbSpace-linux.AppImage`, or `QurbSpace-linux.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu |
| Windows 10 and 11 | `QurbSpace-windows-setup.exe`, tested on GitHub's Windows machines, not yet on a person's PC |

## Known gaps

Unchanged from beta.12: deleting is for good; restoring a whole WhatsApp
backup to a new phone is not proven; Windows' window has not been tried by a
person. New: fast transfers go from the phone to the computer only, not yet the
other way; on Windows they are built and not yet tried on a PC. A file waiting in Google Drive
reaches the phone at its next sync: Drive cannot wake a phone.

Something wrong? [Report it](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace/issues/new/choose).
