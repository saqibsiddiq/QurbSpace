# QurbSpace 0.2.0-beta.10

The first open beta. Installed copies of beta.8 and beta.9 update themselves
to this one.

## New

- **Works from anywhere.** Away from home the phone and computer now find
  each other through IPv6, punching through home routers, and — when nothing
  else gets through, as on Jio's mobile network — the public Nostr relays or
  Tor, still encrypted end to end.
- **Google Drive as a mailbox** (optional). When your devices cannot reach
  each other — the laptop asleep, say — they leave each other files, sealed,
  in a hidden folder of your Drive. Choose the account during setup or in
  Settings; your computer gets the same account from the phone, without
  signing in there.
- **Tap to connect.** Home's button connects the phone: while the
  notification says *Connected*, a file sent from the computer arrives in
  seconds (11 s measured, at home). Tap the notification to disconnect.
- **Nothing syncs on its own** any more: Home's button, or pulling down on any
  screen, does.
- **The apps take less space**: files opened from the computer are kept an
  hour, the mailbox lets go of what it does not need, and the index stays
  compact.

## Changed

- **Recently deleted is gone.** Deleting a file deletes it for good, on every
  device.
- Backup folders are chosen on the phone only; the computer shows what is
  backed up.
- Settings: pause sync, mobile data, backup timing, notification kinds,
  updates, app lock, received files, and resetting a device.

## Fixed

- A phone set up again could be offered an old key from its Google backup,
  with no way to join its computer.
- Sending from the phone could wait 25 s for the computer to notice.
- A phone backing up thousands of files held the app up for seconds at a time.
- The backup progress bar now moves as files arrive.

## Known gaps

Deleting is for good; restoring a whole WhatsApp backup to a new phone is not
proven; Windows has not been run; Google Drive is limited to the accounts
added as testers until Google verifies the app. Full list:
[README](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace#known-gaps).

Something wrong? [Report it](https://github.com/saqibsiddiq/QurbSpace/issues/new/choose).
